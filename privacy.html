RoomBoard

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026

RoomBoard is a tablet display app that shows room and event schedules for shared spaces such as meeting rooms, classrooms, coworking spaces, and community halls. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

What data the app collects

RoomBoard does not collect any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no registration, and no backend server.

The only information stored by the app is saved locally on the device itself:

  • Room name and venue name you enter during setup
  • Your chosen display preferences (theme, time format, refresh interval, etc.)
  • The ICS calendar feed URL you configure, if any
  • A short-lived cache of calendar events for offline display

None of this information is sent to us or any third party.

Calendar data

If you configure an ICS/iCal feed URL, the app fetches calendar events directly from that URL on your device. The events are displayed on screen and temporarily cached on the device for offline use. Calendar data is never transmitted to our servers because we operate no servers.

The ICS URL you enter is stored only on the device. If you use a private calendar URL (such as a Google Calendar "secret address"), keep that URL confidential — anyone with it can read your calendar.

Camera

The app requests camera permission only if you choose to scan a QR code to enter a calendar URL. Camera access is used in that moment only and no images or video are stored or transmitted.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no analytics SDKs, crash reporters, advertising networks, or tracking pixels.

Third-party services

The app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-sharing services. The only network requests the app makes are:

  • Fetching your configured ICS calendar feed URL (device-initiated, no intermediary)

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because it collects no personal data at all, it is suitable for use in environments that serve children (schools, community halls, etc.).

Data deletion

All app data is stored locally on the device. To delete it, use Settings → Advanced → Reset all app data within the app, or uninstall the app. This permanently removes all settings and cached schedules from the device.

Changes to this policy
