RoomBoard

Turn any tablet into a room schedule sign. Mount it by the door and it shows what's on now, what's next, and whether the room is free.

What it does

  • Reads any calendar. Point it at an ICS feed — Google, Apple, Outlook, Nextcloud, or anything else that publishes one.
  • Readable from across a room. Large type on a dark panel, designed to be scanned in passing rather than read up close.
  • Keeps working offline. The last schedule stays on screen if the Wi-Fi drops, and says so.
  • Respects privacy. Choose how much detail passers-by see. Meetings marked private always show as “Reserved”.
  • No accounts, no backend. Settings live on the tablet. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Where it fits

  • Meeting rooms, boardrooms and huddle spaces
  • Classrooms, studios and clinic rooms
  • Event spaces, community halls and coworking desks
RoomBoard is in testing on Android. It is not on Google Play yet — get in touch if you would like to try it on a tablet.
