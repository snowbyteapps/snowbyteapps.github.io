Privacy Policy

Unpocketed for Android  ·  Last updated: 30 August 2026

Unpocketed keeps track of money that is yours but has not come back to you yet. That is sensitive information, so the app is built so that we never see it.

In short. Unpocketed collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of ours for anything to be sent to. What you track stays on your phone, and is included in your phone's own Google backup unless you turn that off.

What the app stores

Everything you enter is written to a database on your own device and stays there. That includes:

  • amounts and currencies
  • who owes the money or is holding it
  • what it is for, and any note or reference number you add
  • expected dates and deadlines
  • whether something was received, returned, kept or missed

None of it is transmitted to us. We operate no server that receives it, so there is nothing for us to read, sell, lose or be compelled to hand over.

Android backup

Unpocketed is included in Android's own backup, the same mechanism that restores your apps when you set up a new phone. This means what you track survives a lost or replaced device, which matters because the app has no cloud account to restore from.

That backup is between your device and your own Google account. It is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours, and we have no access to it. You can turn it off in Android Settings › Google › Backup, or by switching off backup for Unpocketed specifically. If you turn it off, your data stays only on the device and is lost if the device is.

Notifications

The app asks for permission to send notifications so it can remind you before a deadline passes or when money you are owed is late. The reminders are worked out on your device from what you have entered. Nothing is sent anywhere to produce them, and declining the permission does not otherwise limit the app.

Purchases

Unpocketed is free to use for up to three tracked items at a time. Unpocketed Pro removes that limit for a single one-time purchase. There is no subscription.

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your name, card details or billing address; the app only ever learns whether this device is entitled to Pro. Google's handling of the transaction is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Analytics and advertising

There are none. No analytics library, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no access to your device's advertising ID. We collect no usage statistics of any kind, which also means we cannot tell how anyone uses the app.

Permissions

The app requests only what it needs to do its job:

  • Notifications - to send you reminders.
  • Internet and network state - used solely by Google Play Billing. Your tracked money is never sent over it.
  • Run at startup, wake lock, foreground service - so scheduled reminders survive a restart and can run in the background.

Unpocketed does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, files, calendar, SMS, or bank accounts.

Children

Unpocketed is a general-purpose tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect anything from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Unpocketed removes the database and everything in it from your device. Clearing the app's storage in Android Settings does the same without uninstalling.

Because we hold nothing, there is no account to close and no deletion request to send us. If you have Android backup switched on, a copy may persist in your Google account until Android removes it; you can delete that through your Google account settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes to what the app does with your information will also be described in the app's release notes.
